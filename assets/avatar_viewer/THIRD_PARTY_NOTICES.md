# Third-party assets

The Amica assets in `assets/amica` are distributed under the MIT License.
The original license text is retained at `assets/amica/LICENSE`.

The avatar viewer uses Three.js, @pixiv/three-vrm and
@pixiv/three-vrm-animation from jsDelivr at runtime. An internet connection
is required for those JavaScript modules. The VRM model and selected VRMA
animation files are bundled locally with the APK.
